Subscriptions

All purchases are processed securely by Apple or Google. We do not store your payment information. JU Talk offers two subscription options to unlock premium questions and features:

  • Weekly subscription: Renews automatically every 7 days.
  • Yearly subscription: Renews automatically every year.
  • Manage or cancel:
    • iOS: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions
    • Android: Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions
  • Refunds: Refunds are handled exclusively by Apple/Google under their respective policies. We do not have the ability to issue refunds.
Request an Apple Refund Request a Google Play Refund

FAQ

1) Premium access not activated after purchase
Check that your subscription is active: iOS → Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. Android → Google Play Store → Profile → Payments & subscriptions. If active, fully close and reopen the app.

2) App feels slow or stuck
Force close and reopen the app, check your connection, and ensure you're on the latest version from the App Store / Google Play.

3) Change language
Go to Settings → Language and choose your preferred language.

4) Report an inappropriate question
Use the in-app report option on the question screen or email us with a screenshot.

What to include when contacting us

  • Device (e.g., iPhone 14 / Pixel 7)
  • OS version (e.g., iOS 18.x / Android 14)
  • App version (Settings → About)
  • Steps to reproduce (1–2–3)
  • Screenshots or a short screen recording (if possible)
